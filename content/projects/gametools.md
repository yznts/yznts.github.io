The first one moves Genshin Impact exploration progress between the AppSample and HoYoLAB interactive maps, in either direction — chests, oculi, waypoints, shrines and quests. Neither map offers import or export, so it pairs them by position: it fits the coordinate transform between the two from the data itself, then matches each location with the nearest point of the same category, and reports what it could not place rather than guessing."
