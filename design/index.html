About

I've spent the last decade writing software in one form or another — backends in Go, frontends in TypeScript, and lately a fair amount of Swift. I work best on small teams or alone, on problems where the right answer is "ship less, more carefully." Based in Kraków.

Outside the keyboard: photography, long walks, and an ongoing argument with my espresso machine.
