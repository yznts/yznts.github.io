Bulb

macOS 13+ Linux v1.4

A menu-bar controller for Bluetooth LED panels. Pick a color, save scenes, schedule fades — without ever opening a janky vendor app. Bulb talks to the panel directly over BLE and remembers what you like.

Babel

macOS 13+ v2.1

A keyboard-first translation app that lives one shortcut away. Hit a hotkey, type anywhere, get a translation that respects context — without leaving your current window. Works offline for the languages you pin.
